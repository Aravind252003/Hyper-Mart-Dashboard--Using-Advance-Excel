# HyperMart Sales Dashboard

## Project Overview
This HyperMart Sales Dashboard was developed using Microsoft Excel to analyze sales performance, profit trends, customer segments, and regional contributions.

The dashboard provides valuable business insights that help in tracking sales growth, identifying top-performing regions, and monitoring profitability.

## Key Metrics
- Total Sales
- Total Profit
- Total Orders
- Total Customers
- Average Order Value
- Regional Performance Analysis

## Dashboard Insights

### Sales by Region
- West region generated the highest sales.
- Central region showed comparatively lower sales performance.

### Profit Analysis
- Technology category contributed the highest profit.
- Certain sub-categories experienced lower profit margins.

### Sales by Segment
- Consumer segment contributed the largest share of sales.
- Corporate and Home Office segments followed.

### Monthly Sales Trend
- Sales fluctuated across months with noticeable peak seasons.
- Dashboard helps identify high-performing months.

### Top Performing States
- Top 5 states contributed significantly to overall revenue.
- State-wise analysis highlights key business markets.

### Order & Return Analysis
- Returned orders were analyzed to understand their impact on sales and profit.
- Helps identify areas requiring operational improvement.
